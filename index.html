SnapNote - Quick Web Notes Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

1. Overview

SnapNote - Quick Web Notes is a browser extension that helps users capture, save, organize, and retrieve useful information while browsing the web.

This Privacy Policy explains what information SnapNote accesses, how that information is used, and how it is stored.

2. Information SnapNote Handles

SnapNote allows users to save different types of content while browsing, including:

  • Screenshots
  • Custom area screenshots
  • Selected text
  • Copied text
  • Images
  • Webpage links
  • Saved webpages
  • Quick notes
  • Voice note attachments
  • Page titles
  • URLs and domains
  • Timestamps associated with saved content

This information is handled only to provide the features of the SnapNote extension.

3. Local Data Storage

SnapNote is designed with a local-first approach.

Your saved screenshots, images, text, links, webpages, notes, voice notes, and related metadata are stored locally in your browser using browser storage technologies such as IndexedDB.

Your saved content is not uploaded to a SnapNote server or cloud storage as part of the normal saving workflow.

4. No Account Required

SnapNote does not require you to create an account to use its core features.

SnapNote does not require your name, email address, password, payment information, or social media account for normal use.

5. No Tracking or Analytics

SnapNote does not use third-party analytics or tracking services to monitor your browsing activity.

SnapNote does not sell your browsing information or saved content.

6. Webpage Access

SnapNote may access information from the webpage you are currently viewing when you explicitly use features that require webpage access.

This may include actions such as:

  • Capturing a screenshot
  • Capturing a custom area
  • Saving selected text
  • Saving a webpage
  • Saving a link
  • Using browser right-click actions
  • Saving information from the current page

SnapNote uses this access only to perform the feature requested by the user.

7. Browser Permissions

SnapNote requests browser permissions that are required to provide its functionality.

Active Tab

Used to access the currently active webpage when the user performs actions such as screenshot capture, custom area capture, saving a webpage, or saving content from the current tab.

Context Menus

Used to provide right-click options for saving selected text, links, and webpages.

Storage

Used to store extension settings and user preferences.

Unlimited Storage

Used to store user-created screenshots, images, notes, saved text, links, and other captured content locally without the standard extension storage quota.

Scripting

Used when SnapNote needs to interact with the current webpage for capture and content-saving features.

Clipboard Read

Used when the user chooses to paste copied text or images into SnapNote notes.

Tabs

Used to access information such as the active tab's title and URL when saving webpages, links, screenshots, and other captured content.

8. How Your Data Is Used

Information handled by SnapNote is used to provide features such as:

  • Capturing screenshots
  • Capturing custom areas
  • Saving selected text
  • Saving webpages and links
  • Creating and editing notes
  • Attaching screenshots, images, links, and voice notes
  • Searching saved content
  • Organizing saved content
  • Downloading saved content
  • Importing and exporting notes
  • Backing up and restoring saved data

SnapNote does not use your saved content for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

9. Data Sharing

SnapNote does not sell, rent, or share your saved content or browsing information with third parties.

Your locally stored content is not transmitted to SnapNote's developer or a third-party cloud service as part of the normal saving workflow.

10. Data Security

SnapNote keeps saved content within your browser's local storage environment.

Because the data is stored locally, users should also protect access to their browser and computer. Anyone who has access to your browser profile or computer may potentially be able to access locally stored extension data.

11. Import and Export

SnapNote provides import and export functionality so users can manage and back up their saved content.

Exported files are controlled by the user. If an exported SnapNote file is shared with another person or service, that information may become accessible to whoever receives the file.

12. Data Deletion

Users can manage and delete saved content through the SnapNote interface.

Users should export any content they want to keep before uninstalling the extension.

13. Children's Privacy

SnapNote does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

SnapNote does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information from users.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to SnapNote's features or data practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.

15. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or SnapNote's privacy practices, please contact the developer through the SnapNote project repository.
